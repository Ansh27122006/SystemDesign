The "nines" of availability

Availability is usually promised as a number of nines. Each extra nine is a much harder engineering problem — it shrinks tolerable downtime by 10x. Your live session's tier is highlighted below.

AvailabilityDowntime / yearDowntime / day
90% ("one nine")36.5 days2.4 hours
99% ("two nines")3.65 days14.4 minutes
99.9% ("three nines")8.76 hours1.44 minutes
99.99% ("four nines")52.6 minutes8.6 seconds
99.999% ("five nines")5.26 minutes0.86 seconds
Why redundancy matters: with one server, any failure is a full outage — availability collapses to 0% the moment it goes down. With several servers behind a load balancer, one failure just reduces capacity; traffic reroutes to the survivors and availability barely moves. Turn off "Redundant mode" and kill the single server to see the difference instantly.
