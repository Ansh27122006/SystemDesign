Maintainability

How easily a system can be fixed, changed, and operated over its lifetime — not just built once. Three angles, three scenes: how far a change ripples, how fast you notice something's broken, and how safely you can ship.

These map to the four DORA metrics

A widely used industry framework for measuring how maintainable an engineering system actually is in practice — not from opinion, but from what the pipeline above produces.

DORA metricWhat it meansWhat you just saw
Deployment frequencyHow often changes reach productionEach "Ship a change" click

The throughline: maintainability isn't one thing — it's small blast radius (Scene A) plus fast detection (Scene B) plus safe, frequent shipping (Scene C). Systems that score well on all three are the ones teams can change confidently for years, instead of becoming too risky to touch.
