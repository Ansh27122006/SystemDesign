System Design Lab · Characteristic 5 of 8

Consistency

You have multiple copies of the same data. When one changes, does everyone see the new value right away — or does it take a moment to catch up?

00 · Two philosophies

Strong vs. Eventual

Almost every consistency decision in system design comes down to picking a point between these two extremes.

Strong ConsistencyA single shared notebook. Everyone reads from the same page — the moment something is written, every reader instantly sees it. Safer, but slower to write to.
Eventual ConsistencyA rumor spreading through a room. Everyone eventually hears the same story — but for a little while, some people are still repeating the old version.

Game 4 · Quorum Tuner

The W + R > N trick

N total replicas. Write to W of them. Read from R of them. If W + R is bigger than N, at least one node you read from is guaranteed to have the latest write — mathematically, not just luck.

W + R = 5  |  N = 5  →  overlap NOT guaranteed
